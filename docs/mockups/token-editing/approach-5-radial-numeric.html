They always show a number (e.g. Armour) — visible even at rest for quick reference — and become editable (▲/▼ or tap-to-type) when the token is selected.

Radial at rest: non-selected tokens show their active conditions on the ring at fixed 45° slots — identical positions to the selected state, so nothing shifts when you select.
Numeric slots (×2): rounded-square slots holding a value (e.g. Armour, Ward). Always visible — read someone's armour without opening their token.
Editing numbers: on selection the numeric slot gains ▲/▼ steppers (facing outward so they never overlap the token); tap the number itself to type a value directly.
Conditions still toggle: selected ring shows all condition slots — active glow, inactive greyed; click to apply/remove. Numeric slots are never "off", only their value changes.
Shape = meaning: round = condition (on/off), rounded-square = numeric value. The two are visually distinct at a glance, even shrunk to ring size.
Dense grids: selected token's group sits at a higher z-index and neighbouring rings dim to ~40% while a token is selected, so overlapping suns stay readable.
