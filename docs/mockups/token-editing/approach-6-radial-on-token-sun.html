Warhammer Battle Helper · Static HTML
What changed vs Approach 5: when a token is not selected, its active states and its numbers sit ON the token as a small sun (compact radial cluster overlapping the avatar) — at the same angular positions they'll hold when expanded. On selection the sun fans out into the full ring around the token (all 8 slots, greyed inactive conditions, editable numbers, interactive HP, stat chips).

Levels — how to subtract one: left-click an active levelled state = +1 level; right-click (or long-press on touch) = −1 level, removing it at 0. A small ▼ appears on hover over an active levelled slot as a discoverable fallback.

L-click +level · R-click / ▼ −level · greyed = apply
Rest = sun on token: active states and the two numbers cluster on the avatar at a small radius. Everything stays inside the 50px footprint, so tightly packed tokens never blend.
Select = fan out: the same icons animate outward to the full ring (r=44) at identical angles; greyed inactive conditions and editable number steppers appear. Deselect collapses back onto the token.
Level up / down: left-click an active levelled state = +1; right-click / long-press = −1 (removes at 0). Hover reveals a small ▼ on the slot as a visible fallback.
Numbers always readable: armour / ward show on the token even at rest (see Garrett — armour 5, ward 1, no conditions). Steppers only appear on selection.
Shape = meaning: round = on/off (or levelled) condition, rounded-square = numeric value — distinct even at the tiny on-token size.
Readability trade-off: the sun overlaps the avatar art. Icons use a dark disc backing + thin gold ring so they stay legible over any uploaded image.
