What changed vs Approach 3: (1) On a non-selected token, active conditions are drawn as a compact icon strip inside the token (overlapping the avatar's lower edge) — never radiating outward — so tokens packed side by side stay self-contained and don't blend together. (2) On a selected token, the full ring shows every available condition at once: active ones glow, unselected ones are greyed. Click a greyed slot to activate it, click a lit slot to remove it. The ring is the toggle — no ghost "+" slot, no separate palette.

Greyed slot = tap to apply · lit slot = tap to remove
On-token strip (non-selected): Active conditions sit in a dark tray overlapping the avatar's lower edge. Fixed inside the 50px footprint → neighbouring tokens never blend. Over ~3 icons it collapses to +N.
Full ring (selected): All system-defined states (max 8) appear at fixed 45° slots. Active = glowing, inactive = greyed. The ring doubles as the picker — no separate palette popup.
Toggle by click: Tap a greyed slot to apply that condition; tap a lit slot to remove it (or decrement its level first if levelled). Slot positions are stable, so the same condition is always in the same place.
HP & stats unchanged: thin HP bar always on above the token; on selection it becomes an inline +/− row, and configurable stat chips appear below the ring.
Why on-token when idle: the radial ring is great for editing but visually noisy when many tokens are unselected; collapsing to an inside strip keeps the battle map readable.
>8 state types: the system caps its condition set at 8 slots. If a system needs more, the 8th slot becomes a "more…" entry opening a small grid (rare; keep systems ≤8).
