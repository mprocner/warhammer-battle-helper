How It Works

  • Click on a character tile on the left to view details and highlight nearby characters
  • Character details show HP, Movement, and adjustable Modifier
  • Modifier buttons - Use -10/+10 to adjust attack roll modifier (Range: -60 to +60)
  • Action buttons (⚔️🏹✨📜) - Hover to see action names, click to perform (modifier is applied)
  • Condition icons - Click to toggle states (Prone, Bleeding, Poisoned, etc.)
  • Active states are highlighted with golden border and glow
  • Nearby characters within range are highlighted on the grid
  • Potential targets (enemies in range) pulse with red glow

Available States

  • 🛏️ Prone - Character is on the ground
  • 😵 Stunned - Cannot act this turn
  • 😴 Unconscious - Knocked out
  • 🩸 Bleeding - Taking damage over time
  • 🧪 Poisoned - Suffering from poison
  • 🔥 Ablaze - On fire!
  • 👁️ Blinded - Cannot see
  • 🔇 Deafened - Cannot hear
  • 🕸️ Entangled - Movement restricted
  • 😪 Fatigued - Exhausted
  • 😲 Surprised - Caught off guard
  • 💔 Broken - Morale broken
