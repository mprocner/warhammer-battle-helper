# Getting started

## Your account {#your-account}

1. Click **Register** and enter your email and a password (at least 8 characters).
2. Open the activation link from the email. You cannot log in until you do.
3. Forgot your password? Use the reminder on the login screen. The reset link is valid for
   one hour.

Under **Settings** (click your email in the top right corner; **Logout** is there too) you will
find:
- **Account** — your avatar and signature, the name others see in games,
- **Change Password**,
- **Statistics** — your rolls across all games: count, average, criticals and fumbles,
- **Privacy** — consent to analytics (the site works the same without it).

To delete your account or get a copy of your data, write to contact@playrpg.net from your
account's email address. The privacy policy (playrpg.net/privacy) describes what we store and for how long.

## The game list {#the-game-list}

The home page describes the app. After logging in, go to **Game Rooms**, where you see your games, both the ones you run and the ones you play in. You join
someone else's game in one of two ways: open the game master's invite link, or have the game
master add you by your account email. Either way the game appears on your list. A link opened
while logged out takes you to the login screen. No account yet? Register, activate it from the
email and log in in the same browser, and you land straight in the game. A link that does not
work has been changed or removed by the GM: ask them for a new one. When the list is empty, you can create a game or design
your own system right away.

## Creating a game (game masters) {#creating-a-game-game-masters}

1. Click **Create New Game**.
2. Enter a name and pick a system from the list. The list holds the built-in systems and the
   templates made in the [System Creator](05-system-creator.md), including ones others have
   shared. The language filter narrows the list to templates in the languages you pick. You can
   open a sheet preview of a template before choosing it.
3. Open the game and invite players in the **Players** tab (see
   [For game masters](04-for-game-masters.md)).

## Language {#language}

Change the interface language (English or Polish) in the **General** tab inside a game.
The app remembers your choice in this browser.

## Contact and reports {#contact-and-reports}

The **Contact** page (linked in the footer of every page, and in the **General** tab inside a
game) has a single form. Choose what your message is about:
- **Bug or feedback** — describe what you did, what you expected and what happened,
- **Report content** — content in a game that you believe is illegal or breaks the terms of
  service,
- **Other** — anything else.

You do not need an account. When you are logged in, the form fills in your account email.
Without an email we cannot reply, but the message still arrives.

A content report needs where the content is (for example the game name and a description of the
scene, handout or file), a justification, your name and surname or company name, your email and a
statement that you report in good faith. For a report about child sexual abuse, name and email
are optional. Screenshots cannot be attached in the form: mention that you have one and send it
by email to the address shown on the page. We review reports without delay and let you know what
we decided.

The form accepts a few messages every few minutes from one address. If something goes wrong,
write directly to the email address shown on the Contact page.
