# For players

## Your character {#your-character}

1. In the character list on the left, click **+ Add Character**. The new character is
   visible to you and to the game master.
2. Click the tile to see the short card, or the scroll icon next to the name to open the full
   sheet. Changes save as you go.
3. The arrow on the tile places the character on the map in the first free spot.

The game master can also give you access to a character they created. What the sheet looks
like depends on the system chosen for the game.

## Rolling {#rolling}

- **From the sheet:** click an attribute, a skill or a weapon. If the system has modifiers
  turned on, the app first asks for a bonus or penalty.
- **Without the sheet:** the dice buttons under the chat, or the `/r` command
  (see [Dice and chat](06-dice-and-chat.md)).
- **Who sees the result:** the dropdown at the end of the dice row: Everyone, GM + Player
  (you and the GM), GM Only, or one chosen player.
- **My rolls:** hides everyone else's rolls from the log so you can find yours.

The visibility choice applies to rolls from the sheet too.

## On the map {#on-the-map}

- Move your tokens with **Select / Move**.
- **Measure distance** shows the distance in game units. Others see your ruler.
- **Drawing** lets you mark something on the map for the whole table.
- If a token shows states or bars (for example health), you can change them right on your
  character's token.
- You only see the scene the game master moved you to.

## Handouts and notes {#handouts-and-notes}

- **Handouts** are letters, portraits, maps and documents from the game master. You only see
  the ones shared with you or with everyone. Clicking one opens it in its own window.
- **Notes** are private by default. In the editor you can mark a note public to share it with
  the table. You see your own notes and other people's public ones.

## Your in-game settings {#your-in-game-settings}

Click your avatar in the top bar to set:
- your avatar (from your account, from your character, or uploaded),
- your signature, the name shown next to your log entries (from your account, from your
  character, or your own),
- the size of your avatar in the bar,
- your colour.

### Your colour {#your-colour}

Every participant has their own colour. It shows as the outline of your avatar in the bar, on
your ruler and pings on the map, as a bar next to your log entries and along the top edge of the
pop-up with your roll. Drawing starts in your colour too; a colour you pick by hand in the
drawing toolbar stays for the rest of the session.

You get a colour when you join a game: the first one from the palette nobody has yet. In the
settings you can pick another palette colour or any colour with **Custom colour**. Two people
may share a colour, and the window tells you who already has it. You set the colour separately
in each game.

In the **General** tab you set your own music volume (it only changes the sound on your end),
the language and the control scheme. **Back to Game List** and **Leave Game** are there too.

### Game look {#game-look}

The GM picks the colours, fonts and window frames of the game (the theme: Parchment, Sci-fi or
1920s). If the theme does not suit you, for example it is hard for you to read, turn on
**Always use the Parchment theme** in the **General** tab. It only changes what you see, in all
your games, and the same switch is in your account settings under **Game**.
