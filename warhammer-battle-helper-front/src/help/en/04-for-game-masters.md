# For game masters

## Players {#players}

You invite players in the **Players** tab in two ways: with a link or by email. This is also
where you remove a player from the game. Their character stays, so you can hand it to someone
else.

### Invite link {#invite-link}

Click **Create link**, then **Copy**, and paste the link wherever your group talks, e.g. on
Discord. Anyone who opens it joins the game as a player, without you having to approve them.
Someone who is not logged in logs in or registers first and then lands straight in the game.
You see the new player right away, because they appear on the list.

A game has one link, with no expiry and no use limit. **New link** replaces it, and the old one
stops working. **Remove link** turns the invitation off. Either way, players who already joined
stay in the game. Share the link only where you want people to join.

### Invite by email {#invite-by-email}

Type the email of the player's account. The player is added right away: nothing is sent and
they do not have to confirm anything.

## Characters and NPCs {#characters-and-npcs}

- The **NPC List** on the left is visible only to you.
- **Manage Visibility** decides which players can open a character's sheet.
- **Clone** makes several copies at once, for example a squad of guards.
- On a token you can hide the character from players (**Hide from players**). A hidden token
  is seen only by people with access to the sheet, and you see it dimmed. You can also mark a
  character as **Killed**.

## Scenes {#scenes}

In the **Scenes** tab:
1. Create a scene: a name and a grid size (up to 50×50).
2. Add a background: drag an image from the **Files** tab onto the map.
3. In the scene settings, turn the grid and fog on or off, link **scene music**, and
   **assign players**. If the grid is hard to see on the map, change its **grid colour**; the
   button next to it restores the default. A player always sees exactly one scene, the one you assigned them to.

You can work on a scene your players cannot see yet and show it when it is ready.

**Map settings** (the **General** tab) apply to the whole game: how much one square is worth,
which unit to measure in, and whether tokens snap to the grid.

**GM layer.** Images on the GM layer are visible only to you, for example notes on the map or
hidden traps. Move an image between layers from its right-click menu.

**Fog of war.** The **Fog of War** tool hides and reveals the map with a brush, a rectangle or
a line. You can undo the last stroke, reveal everything or clear the fog. To you the fog is
see-through (there is an opacity slider), to players it is solid.

## Tokens: states and bars {#tokens-states-and-bars}

A token can show a ring of states (for example bleeding, stunned) with levels, counters and
bars (for example health). Left-click a state to raise its level, right-click to lower it.
Counters show on an unselected token too, even at 0; a state shows only while it is active.
Every element can be hidden from players, and a bar can hide just its numbers. The layout for
all characters is set in the System Creator (**Token display**), and you can fine-tune a single
token in its configuration.

An image token can have a caption under it, like a character's name. Type it into the **Label**
field of the token's configuration (the gear next to the selected token); an empty field removes it.

## Handouts {#handouts}

The **Handout** button in the tab header (or **Add Handout** inside a folder) uploads an image, a PDF or a text file and asks who should see it: everyone or
chosen players. Nothing is shared until you choose. Organise handouts in folders and drag them
to change the order.

## Files {#files}

The **Files** tab is your image library: maps, tokens, portraits. It is shared across all your
games. Organise files in folders, crop, rename and delete them. Before deleting, the app shows
which games use the file.

## Music {#music}

In the **Music** tab you upload tracks (MP3, WAV) and organise them in folders and playlists
(tavern, battle, travel…). Everyone hears playback at the same moment, and a player who joins
mid-track hears it from the right spot. The volume slider in this tab changes the volume for
the whole table. Each player also has their own slider.

## Minigames {#minigames}

The **Games** tab: pick Yahtzee or dice poker and the players who are online. The board pops up
for everyone, the chosen players take turns and the rest watch. One minigame runs at a time.
If the server restarts, a running minigame ends.

## Template and sync {#template-and-sync}

A game uses the copy of the system template from the moment it was created. After changing the
template in the creator, click **Sync** on the game's card in the game list. Values of fields
removed from the template will be lost.

## Game theme {#game-theme}

In the **General** tab, under **Appearance**, you pick the game's theme: **Parchment** (the
default), **Sci-fi**, **1920s** or **Dark fantasy**. The theme changes the colours, fonts and window frames of
the whole game view, character sheets included, and everyone at the table sees it at once. The
game list and the start page stay in Parchment. A player who does not like the theme can switch
back to Parchment for themselves.
